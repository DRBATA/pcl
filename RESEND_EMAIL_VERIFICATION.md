# Resend Email Verification Setup

## What needs to be done

The contact form on prostatecare.co.uk sends emails via Resend. To make it work, **Claire Lloyd's email address must be verified in the Resend account**.

## Steps to verify the email

1. **Go to Resend dashboard**: https://resend.com/emails
2. **Click "Senders"** in the left sidebar
3. **Click "Add Sender"** or **"Add Domain"**
4. **Choose "Email Address"** (not domain verification)
5. **Enter**: `claire.lloyd@prostatecare.co.uk`
6. **Resend will send a verification email** to that address
7. **Click the verification link** in the email from Resend
8. **Done** — the email is now verified and the contact form will work

## What happens after verification

Once verified:
- Website visitors can submit the contact form
- Emails arrive in Claire's inbox at `claire.lloyd@prostatecare.co.uk`
- Visitors' replies go to their own email (set in the form)
- Brian Lynch is CC'd on all emails

## Timeline

- Verification email arrives within seconds
- Contact form works immediately after clicking the verification link
- No DNS changes needed
- No domain ownership required

## Questions?

If the verification email doesn't arrive, check:
- Spam/junk folder
- Email address is spelled correctly: `claire.lloyd@prostatecare.co.uk`

Contact Resend support if verification link expires or doesn't work.
